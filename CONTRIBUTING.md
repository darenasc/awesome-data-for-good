# Contributing to Awesome Data for Good

First off, thanks for taking the time to contribute! ❤️

## Send your favorite Data for Good stuff

What we are looking for? Data for Good...

- Books
- Courses
- Data Science for Social Good Summer Programs
- Ethics
- Events
- Funding
- Global projects
- Organisations
- Readings
- Research
  - Workshops
- Resources
  - Tools and libraries (Python, R, Julia, etc.)
- Frameworks, Guidelines, and Standards

## How to contribute?

Feel free to recommend one or more Data for Good resources.

- Open an [Issue](https://github.com/darenasc/awesome-data-for-good/issues/new).
- Provide a **name**, an **URL** and a brief **description** (ideally taken from the source) about your recommendation(s).
- Provide a **recommended section** to include it in the [awesome-data-for-good](https://darenasc.github.io/awesome-data-for-good/) website.

I will then take care of the issue as soon as possible.

> ### Other ways to contribute
> And if you like the project, but just don't have time to contribute, that's fine. There are other easy ways to support the project and show your appreciation, which we would also be very happy about:
